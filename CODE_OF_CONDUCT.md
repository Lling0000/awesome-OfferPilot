# Code Of Conduct

OfferPilot is built for people handling stressful, private job-search work. The contributor community should be practical, respectful, and careful with user data.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be direct, kind, and specific in technical feedback.
- Assume contributors are working with incomplete context until the evidence says otherwise.
- Keep disagreement focused on code, docs, product tradeoffs, and user safety.
- Use fictional data in examples, tests, screenshots, and reports.
- Respect boundaries around resumes, interview notes, compensation, identity, and job-search history.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing or requesting private candidate data.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, cookies, or credentials.
- Pressuring contributors to reveal employment, compensation, immigration, health, or identity details.
- Presenting unsupported claims as source-backed facts.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, maintainer spaces, community chats, demos, and any public venue where someone represents OfferPilot.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers through the least public channel available. Security or privacy incidents should follow `SECURITY.md`; do not open a public issue with sensitive details.

Reports should include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- Who was involved.
- Any links, screenshots, or logs that can be shared without exposing private data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, ask for edits, close issues, block accounts, or limit participation to protect users and contributors. Enforcement should be proportional, documented among maintainers when possible, and oriented toward a safer project.
